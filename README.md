## Hey, I'm Ulysse

MS in ECE at Carnegie Mellon University. I design boards and bring them up: schematic, layout, power, then the firmware that proves they work. More and more, that firmware runs a learned model.

At CMU's MetaMobility Lab I train the model that drives a knee exoskeleton, run it on the exo's microcontroller, and work on sensing hardware for the lab's exoskeletons. At Tornyol (YC F25) I designed a 22×22 mm Betaflight flight controller and a 20 A supercapacitor charger for autonomous drones, brought up a lidar on an STM32N6, and modeled its noise in simulation so the flight policy could fly outdoors. At Withings I built a low-power wake-on-sound pipeline for a smartwatch that cut a snoring-detection feature's battery cost from 15 days to 1.7.

I also write software: Python and OpenCV vision pipelines on the Withings production line, the XRP Ledger side of our Capital One prize at TartanHacks 2026, and a declarative NixOS fleet running five machines.
